· 请注意：客服对话、语音朗读与语音输入从这一批起计入 token（服务端已上线，装旧版也一样）。免费档每天有额度上限。

QiMeng 2.51

· Reporting now has a “non-consensual intimate imagery” reason. If the person depicted is you, the dialog points you to the faster route — ideahubs.org/takedown — no account needed, handled within 48 hours, and we also search our site for identical copies.
· When generation is blocked, it now says why: today’s allowance is used up (resets at 00:00 UTC), your balance is short, or that tier needs a higher plan — until now these surfaced as an error you could not read, sometimes in Chinese on an English screen.
· If a refund ever clawed tokens back, your profile now states how much is still owed and that your next top-up settles it automatically; the cost line on generation says the same thing instead of quoting a normal price and failing on tap.
· Please note: support chat, speech playback and voice input now consume tokens (server-side, so this applies to older builds too). The free tier has a daily allowance.
