启梦 2.47

· 同一台手机换账号不再串号：草稿、剪到一半的成片、待取回的成片、本机模板、收藏等都按账号分开，换号后看不到上一个账号的东西。
· 升级后第一个登录的账号会接管这台手机上原有的草稿和剪辑稿；几个人共用的手机，请先用原来的账号登录一次。
· 出片、推演、上传、合成还在进行时退出登录，会提示你先等它完成。
· AI 客服：点 🔇 当场静音；回答到一半断网不再卡住。
· 相机拍照失败一次后不再整个用不了；QQ 登录期间拍的照片不会再丢。
· 取回成片更稳：简约模式下取回的也会存进草稿，并告诉你取回的结果。
· 修了一些小问题：上传失败的原因说得更准、同名用户也能举报和拉黑、Live2D 封面传完才能发布、推演过的段删除前会先确认等。

QiMeng 2.47

· Switching accounts on the same phone no longer mixes data: drafts, half-edited videos, videos waiting to be retrieved, local templates, bookmarks and more are kept per account.
· After upgrading, the first account to log in takes over the drafts and half-edited videos already on this phone. If several people share the phone, log in with the original account first.
· Logging out while a video, plan, upload or merge is still running now asks you to wait until it finishes.
· AI support: tapping 🔇 mutes it right away, and losing the connection mid-answer no longer leaves it stuck.
· One failed photo no longer breaks the camera for the rest of the session, and photos taken during QQ sign-in are no longer lost.
· Retrieving a video is more reliable: in Simple mode it's saved to drafts too, and you're told how it went.
· Smaller fixes: clearer upload error reasons, users with the same name can be reported and blocked, Live2D models can't be published before the cover finishes uploading, and deleting a planned segment asks first.
