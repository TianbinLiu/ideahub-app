启梦 2.61

· 新技能「按主题改写全片剧本」：写一句你的主题，模型把每一段的剧情换成你的故事——段数、镜头、时长、卡位都不动，逐段对照、点了才写回。在画布「/」面板里；按别人的制作过程做同款时，也能在选角那一步直接填主题。
· 按别人的制作过程做同款，现在可以「接在我现在的流水线后面」，不必整条替换。
· 同款奖励：别人照着你的作品做了同款并公开发布满一段时间，平台会奖励你 token（数额、上限与要挂多久都写在发布页）。到账时消息页会通知你。
· 模板提取只保留白模两条路线；以前做的经典配方模板照常能用。

QiMeng 2.61

· New skill “Rewrite the whole script to a theme”: write your theme in one sentence and the model turns every segment's plot into your story — segment count, shots, durations and card slots stay put; you compare segment by segment and nothing is written back until you tap. Find it in the canvas “/” panel, or fill in a theme right at the casting step when remixing someone's process.
· When remixing someone's process you can now “append after my current pipeline” instead of replacing it.
· Remix rewards: when someone else remixes your video and their remix stays public for a while, the platform rewards you tokens (the amount, the limits and how long it must stay public are shown on the publish page). You get a notification when it arrives.
· Template extraction now offers only the two blockout routes; classic-recipe templates you made earlier still work.
