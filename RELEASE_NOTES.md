启梦 2.59

· 作品页新增「查看制作过程」：作者公开之后，别人能看到这条片是怎么一段段做出来的（每段的剧情、镜头、档位、时长、卡组），并点「⚡ 按这个流程做同款」把整条流水线铺到自己的草稿里——先选角（原作的卡用不用、空位填谁），再一段一段自己出片。
· 发布页多了「公开制作过程」开关（默认开）：第一次点「发布」会先给你看一屏「别人会看到这些」。你的原话、没选中的方案、圈选、导演台、上传的参考视频都不会给出去；声明过真实人物的卡与从别人那儿装来的卡只留一个空位。
· 编辑页新增「制作过程」：存量作品可以从留存的工坊工程补公开，随时取消公开或删除；回炉之后旧的制作过程会自动对外隐藏，可以用新一版重新公开。
· 同款署名：按别人的流程或做同款发出去的作品，作品页会写「按 @谁 的流程做的同款」并链回原作；原作页显示「N 人按这条的流程做了同款」。

QiMeng 2.59

· Video pages have a new “View process”: once the author shares it, others can see how the video was made segment by segment (each segment's plot, shot, tier, duration and cards) and tap “⚡ Remake with this process” to lay the whole pipeline out in their own draft — cast first (keep the original's cards or not, fill the open slots), then render segment by segment.
· The publish page has a new “Share the process” switch (on by default): the first time you tap “Publish” you see what others will see. Your own wording, unchosen proposals, markups, the director stage and uploaded reference videos are never shared; cards declared as real people and cards installed from others only leave an open slot.
· The edit page has a new “Process” section: share the process of an existing video from its retained studio project, unshare or delete it any time; after a remake the old process is hidden automatically and you can share the new version.
· Remix credit: a video remade with someone's process (or via “Remake”) shows “Remade with @author's process” linking back to the original, and the original shows how many people remade it.
